# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

An interactive Power BI business intelligence dashboard analyzing workforce patterns, turnover rates, and retention drivers across corporate departments.

![Dashboard Preview](assets/dashboard_preview.png)

---

## Business Problem
Employee turnover incurs significant organizational costs through recruiting, onboarding, and lost domain knowledge. The goal of this analysis is to identify key drivers behind employee departure and provide human resources leadership with data-driven insights to boost retention.

---

## Key Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Headcount:** 1,470 employees
* **Active Workforce:** 1,233 employees
* **Attrition Count:** 237 departures
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.1%

---

## Core Findings & Insights
1. **Overtime is the Leading Attrition Driver:**
   * Employees working regular overtime experience an attrition rate of **30.5%**, compared to just **10.4%** for non-overtime staff. Overtime workers account for more than 53% of all departures despite representing a smaller fraction of the company.
2. **Departmental Vulnerabilities:**
   * **Sales** experienced the highest turnover rate at **20.6%**, followed by **Human Resources** at **19.0%** and **Research & Development** at **13.8%**.
3. **High-Risk Roles:**
   * **Laboratory Technicians (62)**, **Sales Executives (57)**, and **Research Scientists (47)** represent the largest volume of departures.
4. **Early-Career Drop-Off:**
   * Turnover spikes heavily during the **1st year** of employment (39 departures) and levels off significantly after year 10, highlighting a critical need for structured onboarding and first-year retention initiatives.

---

## Recommendations for Leadership
* **Overtime Policy Audits:** Implement workload reviews and fatigue-mitigation thresholds in high-churn technical and sales roles.
* **Onboarding & Mentorship:** Focus retention and milestone check-ins on employees within their first 12–24 months.
* **Role-Specific Career Progression:** Provide clear promotion roadmaps for entry-level lab and research staff to reduce role attrition.

---

## Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop:** Interactive visualization, report design, and UI cards.
* **DAX:** Calculated measures for attrition rates, retention percentages, and conditional aggregations.
* **Power Query:** Data transformation, column profiling, and type formatting.
